## Direction (binding for Tasks P2-4/5/6)

- **Spend the boldness in one place: the Today screen.** It is the landing page after sign-in: "Good morning, Tanav. Three things need you." — a personal, ranked list (tickets waiting on me, unassigned queue, devices due back, requests to approve) with one-key actions, and the assistant's orb breathing beside a one-line briefing it wrote. One orchestrated 300 ms staggered entrance here, nowhere else.
- **Everything else is instant.** Palette, shortcuts, list navigation: zero animation (Emil rule: 100+ times a day). Popovers scale from their trigger with `cubic-bezier(0.23,1,0.32,1)` ≤200 ms; modals 200–250 ms centered; buttons `scale(0.96)` on press, `transition: transform 160ms ease-out`; exits softer than enters; theme switch snaps (transition suppression + reflow); concentric radii (outer = inner + padding); shadows for depth, borders for structure; icons `currentColor`, stroke matched to text weight; hover only under `(hover:hover) and (pointer:fine)`; reduced motion keeps opacity/colour only.
- **Identity, not template.** IBM Plex Sans/Mono stays (a choice, not a default). Palette stays navy + brass but brass becomes a signature: a single warm edge-light on the one focused element (the selected row, the active panel), never a wash. Login gets a real hero: the school and the team, a live count from the directory ("4,278 devices, 3,709 people, one queue"), and the sign-in form — no empty slab. No all-caps labels, no middle dots, no arrows in text, no numbered markers unless it is a sequence.
- **Convenience everywhere ("stuff just happens").** Paste anything → the palette recognises an asset tag, serial, OSIS, email or ticket number and jumps. Typing a requester pulls their room, class and assigned devices; one keystroke links the device. Intake drafts category and priority as you type (heuristics; AI when connected) and warns about a likely duplicate ("EDT-1042, same room, same issue, open"). Queue rows claim and resolve inline; j/k moves, c claims, r opens resolve, o opens. Resolve pre-drafts the solution from the timeline. Notifications carry their action (Claim, Open). Scan → device → assign/return in one tap. Saved views ("Room 214", "Chromebook batteries"). Empty states say what to do next.
- **The assistant feels alive and useful.** Orb states as ruled; streaming with reasoning summary; tool chips that read like a colleague ("Linked A-93542614", "Resolved EDT-1042"); proactive briefing on Today; "Ask about this ticket" pre-fills context; push-to-talk; on phone a full sheet with the composer pinned. Never asks first except for admin tools.
- **Copy** in the interface's voice: plain verbs, sentence case, buttons say what happens, errors say what to do, the same word through a whole flow.
